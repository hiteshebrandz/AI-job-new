## FORMATTING GUIDELINES FOR CHANDIGARH UNIVERSITY

### Page Setup
- **Paper Size:** A4 (210 × 297 mm)
- **Margins:** Top: 1.5 inches, Bottom: 1 inch, Left: 1.5 inches, Right: 1 inch
- **Font:** Times New Roman or Arial, Size 12
- **Line Spacing:** 1.5 for body text, single for footnotes and references
- **Alignment:** Justified

### Heading Hierarchy
- **Title/Chapter Headings:** Bold, 14 pt, 1.5 spacing before and after
- **Section Headings (Level 1):** Bold, 12 pt
- **Subsection Headings (Level 2):** Bold, 12 pt, indented
- **Level 3 Headings:** Italic, 12 pt, indented

### Pagination
- **Cover page, certificates, acknowledgments, abstract:** Roman numerals (i, ii, iii, ...)
- **Main content onwards:** Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3, ...)
- **Page numbers:** Bottom center or top right

### Tables and Figures
- **Captions:** Numbered and titled
- **Placement:** Immediately after first reference in text
- **Source:** Include source for external content
- **Format:** Professional appearance, consistent styling

### Numbering
- Chapters: 1, 2, 3, ...
- Sections: 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 2.2, ...
- Subsections: 1.1.1, 1.1.2, ...
- Figures: Figure 1.1, Figure 2.1, ...
- Tables: Table 1.1, Table 2.1, ...

### Bibliography/References
- **Style:** IEEE, APA, or Harvard (Check with university)
- **Arrangement:** Alphabetical by author surname
- **Formatting:** Hanging indent, 0.5 inches
- **Spacing:** Single spacing within reference, 1.5 spacing between references

### Appendix
- **Labeling:** Appendix A, Appendix B, etc.
- **Numbering:** Figures and tables in appendix: A.1, A.2, B.1, etc.
- **Page numbers:** Continue from main document
